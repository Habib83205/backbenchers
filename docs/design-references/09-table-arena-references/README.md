## Table No. 21 — art direction

- Old wooden school bench, believable scale and subtle hand-worn texture.
- Natural warm late-afternoon sunlight from classroom windows.
- Realistic 2.5D camera angle, readable desk outline and a clean central play zone.
- Visible desk edges for pen fall detection and clear contact shadows.
- Minimal clutter around the active pen-fight surface; peripheral notebooks or stationery can decorate the scene without blocking gameplay.
- Strong visual hierarchy: pens first, desk second, softly blurred classroom third.
- Keep the gameplay physics planar (2D) even with layered perspective.

These user-supplied images are visual references, **not production-ready licensed game art**. Do not copy distinctive third-party compositions or branding. The PNGs should be stored in this folder without image quality changes.
