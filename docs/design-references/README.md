## Game-specific visual direction

- **Game identity:** BACKBENCHERS — school nostalgia with professional, fresh, joyful game UI.
- **First playable arena:** **Table No. 21**, a realistic, slightly worn classroom wooden desk in a recognizable school classroom.
- **Visuals:** layered 2.5D depth, warm daylight, natural materials and subtle camera perspective; gameplay remains 2D physics-driven.
- **Pen arena:** keep pen silhouettes, desk edges and drop zones clear; avoid cluttered controls across the playable desk.
- **UI inspiration split:** use notebook navigation and classroom education cues for menus, progression/streak visuals for retention, collectible roster styling for pens, and concise lobby layouts for modes.
- **Priority:** gameplay clarity, touch and mouse responsiveness, readable labels, accessible contrast, smooth animation, reduced-motion support.
- **Do not** make a generic mobile ad-game interface, lootbox clone, or MMORPG lobby. Mix inspirations selectively into one consistent school-themed design system.

## Proposed game surfaces
